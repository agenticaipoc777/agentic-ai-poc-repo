Tirupati Balaji Temple

The Tirumala Venkateswara Temple, commonly known as Tirupati Balaji
Temple, is a Hindu temple located in the hill town of Tirumala at
Tirupati in Andhra Pradesh, India. It is dedicated to Venkateswara,
a form of the Hindu deity Vishnu.

Tirupati is a city in the Chittoor district of Andhra Pradesh,
southern India. The temple sits atop the Tirumala hills, part of the
larger Seshachalam Hills range.
